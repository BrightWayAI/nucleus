If you're picking up an open proposal, look in the parent `docs/proposals/` directory (everything not in `shipped/`).

## Conventions

- Shipped proposals **stay here**, unchanged. They are the historical record of what was designed.
- The CHANGELOG in each plugin's own repo is the authoritative record of *what shipped*. These proposals are the record of *what was specced*.
- The top-level `CLAUDE.md` in this nucleus repo links to active proposals; shipped ones are referenced by their archive path here.
